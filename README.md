# Titan Company Ltd. – Strategic Profitability & Growth Analysis

## Project Overview

This project presents a consulting-style strategic analysis of Titan Company Ltd. The objective is to understand the key drivers of revenue growth and profitability, evaluate segment performance, benchmark financial performance against selected peers, and assess potential growth and margin scenarios.

The analysis combines financial analysis, business research, Python-based quantitative analysis, Power BI visualization, and strategic recommendations.

## Business Problem

Titan has experienced significant revenue growth over the FY2022–FY2026 period. However, revenue growth and profitability growth have not moved at the same pace.

This project investigates:

- What are the key drivers of Titan's growth?
- Which business segments contribute most to revenue and profitability?
- How have EBITDA and PAT margins evolved?
- How does Titan compare with selected peers?
- What are the implications of different revenue-growth and margin scenarios?
- What strategic actions could support sustainable profitable growth?

## Analytical Approach

The project follows a consulting-style approach:

1. Financial performance analysis
2. Revenue and profitability trend analysis
3. Segment-level analysis
4. Competitive benchmarking
5. Profitability and margin analysis
6. Scenario and sensitivity analysis
7. Strategic interpretation
8. Power BI dashboard development

## Key Financial Findings

For FY2022–FY2026:

- Revenue CAGR: approximately 32.06%
- EBITDA CAGR: approximately 25.64%
- PAT CAGR: approximately 23.26%

The analysis also evaluates changes in EBITDA and PAT margins over the period to identify potential profitability pressures despite strong top-line expansion.
